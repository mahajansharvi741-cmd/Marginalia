Photosynthesis — Detailed Explanation

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants convert light energy into chemical energy, stored as glucose. It happens mainly in the chloroplasts of leaf cells, using a green pigment called chlorophyll to absorb sunlight.

Think of it as a plant's way of "cooking" its own food: it takes in carbon dioxide from air and water from soil, and using sunlight as the energy source, produces glucose (food) and releases oxygen as a byproduct.

☀️ Sunlight absorbed → 💧 CO₂ + H₂O combine → 🌿 Glucose produced → 💨 O₂ released
